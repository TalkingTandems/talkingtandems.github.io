Route Overview

  • Distance: 29 km
  • Grade: Easy to Moderately Difficult

This loop keeps to quiet roads with a couple of short climbs and good-sightline crossings of major roads.

Start and Finish Point

Location: Kingskettle

Short North Fife Loop
29 km Easy – Moderate

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Start: Kingskettle
  2. Head to Freuchie
  3. Continue to Newton of Falkland
  4. Ride into Falkland
  5. Proceed to Dunshalt
  6. Carry on to Auchtermuchty
  7. Climb towards Lumquat Mill
  8. Descend to Rossie
  9. Continue to Charlottetown
  10. Finish: Return to Kingskettle

Lunch Options

  • The Golf Inn at Ladybank (Bunker Café open Wed–Sun, 9:00–14:00).

Important Notes and Warnings

  • The Freuchie → Falkland section has a gentle rise (more an undulation).
  • There’s a steady but not-too-long climb from Auchtermuchty to Lumquat Mill, followed by a fast descent to Rossie.
  • Major road crossings with good sightlines: A92 at Freuchie, A91 at Rossie, and the A92 again just before the finish. Manage with care.
  • Flat option: To avoid the lumpy sections, go directly from Freuchie to Dunshalt on the outward leg, then head straight to Ladybank and the finish. It’s about as flat as Fife gets!
