Route Notes

This is a figure-of-eight loop with Meigle as the crossing point. Two cafés in Meigle make it ideal for a mid-ride stop, but note that:

• One café is shut Monday
• The other is shut Tuesday
→ Therefore this route is best ridden from Wednesday onwards.

Rode with DM on 21/06/24 — a very tough day out indeed. Turning for home at Meigle shortens the route significantly, but the outward leg as far as Abernyte is particularly challenging.

Note: The Strathmore Ride (TTGR51) uses the same start/finish but is significantly easier.
