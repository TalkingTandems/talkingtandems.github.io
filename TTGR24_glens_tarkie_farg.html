Notes

  • Take care crossing the busy A91 when leaving Strathmiglo.
  • The ascent of Glen Tarkie is long but steady; Abernethy Glen descent is fast and narrow.
  • Bridge of Earn → Earn Coffee Shop follows part of the old Perth United CC hilly TT course (in reverse).
  • The exit from Rhynd Road to the A912 is awkward.
  • The Rhynd Road loop is optional.
  • Ascent of Glen Farg: twisting road with double white lines — tandems should stay spaced for safety.
  • Excellent descent Arngask → Gateside; re-cross the A91 with care.
  • Short but tough climb from Gateside to Dryside Road; final descent is usually fast but potholes present.
