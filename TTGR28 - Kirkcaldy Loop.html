Scenery and points of interest

Starting and finishing at Dalgety Bay Sailing Club, the Kirkcaldy Loop offers an enjoyable 47.7 km journey through the towns, villages and countryside of southern Fife. The ride heads through Inverkeithing and Crossgates before continuing towards Mossgreen, Mossmorran and Auchtertool, providing a varied route with plenty of changing surroundings to enjoy along the way.

From Auchtertool, the route continues towards Kirkcaldy, where riders have the opportunity to take a short diversion along the Esplanade for refreshments at the Merchant’s House. This provides a welcome opportunity to relax and enjoy a break before tackling the return journey.

Leaving Kirkcaldy, the route follows Jawbanes Road towards Bernard's Smithy and Braefoot, eventually returning through Dalgety Bay to the Sailing Club. With a mixture of familiar Fife communities and more rural stretches, this moderately difficult loop offers an interesting day out for tandem crews, with plenty of opportunities for pilots to describe the changing surroundings to their stoker.

Terrain and cycling difficulty

The Kirkcaldy Loop is a moderately difficult ride covering just under 48 km through southern Fife. It offers a satisfying outing for tandem crews comfortable with a few hours in the saddle, with a mixture of built-up and more rural sections. Allow time for a relaxed pace, navigation and a refreshments stop.

What to Expect

Distance: At 47.7 km (approximately 29.6 miles), this is a substantial club ride requiring a reasonable level of cycling fitness and stamina.

Climbing: The route is classified as moderately difficult, although the total ascent has not yet been established. Tandem crews should be prepared for some climbing and adjust their effort accordingly.

Descents: Expect to manage changes in gradient along the route. Pilots should select a comfortable speed on descents, particularly where bends or junctions require additional care.

Route character: The journey passes through Inverkeithing, Crossgates, Auchtertool and Kirkcaldy, as well as smaller settlements and connecting roads. Pilots should remain attentive to junctions, changes in surroundings and other road users.

Tandem considerations: Good communication between pilot and stoker is especially valuable when approaching climbs, slowing for junctions or preparing for descents. Clear, early instructions help both riders work together comfortably.

Refreshments and pacing: The Merchant’s House in Kirkcaldy is an option for refreshments, requiring a short out-and-back diversion along the Esplanade. Plan suitable breaks and allow sufficient time for the return journey to Dalgety Bay Sailing Club.
