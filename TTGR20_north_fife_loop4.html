TTGR20 – North Fife Loop 4

Three further North Fife routes, each around 55–65 km and generally Difficult, including scenic climbs, coastal sections and café stops.

Markinch Loop
≈65 km Difficult

Step‑by‑Step Route

  1. Start: Markinch
  2. → Star
  3. → Cadger’s Brae (descent)
  4. → Freuchie
  5. → Newton of Falkland
  6. → Falkland
  7. → Dunshalt
  8. → Auchtermuchty
  9. → Newburgh (Buttercup Café – Lunch)
  10. → Ballenbreich
  11. → Flisk
  12. → Hazelton Walls
  13. → Rathillet
  14. → Cupar
  15. → Ceres
  16. → Craigrothie
  17. → Chance Inn
  18. → Burnturk
  19. → Star
  20. Finish: Markinch

Cupar → St Andrews → Crail Loop
≈55 km Moderately Difficult – Difficult

Step‑by‑Step Route

  1. Start: Cupar
  2. → Pitscottie
  3. → Craigmount
  4. → St Andrews
  5. → Kingsbarns
  6. → Crail (Lunch)
  7. → Higham Toll
  8. → Peat Inn
  9. → Baldinnie
  10. → Ceres
  11. Finish: Cupar

Notes

NB – The latter route is best suited to Spring (just after the change to DST), when tourist levels are lower and the St Andrews → Crail coast road is quieter. An alternative via Stravithie and Dunino is possible, but involves a long climb and the Dunino → Crail road is often used as a fast rat‑run by impatient drivers.
