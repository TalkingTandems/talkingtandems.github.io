Notes

* - This is a reference to an outburst from French cyclist Octave Lapize during the 1910 Tour de France, which was the first to feature mountain stages. Lapize had been reduced to a walk on the ascent of 'L'Aubisque' in the Pyrenees (fixed single speed gears in these days!), when he caught sight of the 'Commissaires' (race referees) and shouted at them: 'Vous etes des assassins, oui! des assassins! (You are murderers, yes! murderers!)'. So 'Le Parcours Des Assassins' translates as 'The Murderers' Course'. The route takes in some of the most demanding climbs in this area (shown in bold), and it will be a strong team indeed that is able to ride it without getting off to push anywhere (unless using E-assist of course…)
