Scenery and points of interest

Starting and finishing at Lochend Farm, this 45.8 km circular ride takes Talking Tandems through an attractive mix of Fife countryside, villages and changing landscapes. The route begins by heading towards Scotlandwell and Kinnesswood, two settlements close to the Lomond Hills, before continuing through Glenlomond and Gateside.

The journey then takes riders through Dunshalt and Newton of Falkland, before continuing towards Leslie and Auchmuirbridge. Along the way, the route moves between lower-lying countryside and more elevated ground, giving the ride a varied character. The changing terrain offers plenty for tandem pilots to describe to their stoker, from the character of the villages to the surrounding hills and farmland.

Returning to Lochend Farm completes an enjoyable circuit through this part of Fife. With its mixture of familiar village names, countryside and some more demanding sections, the ride offers a rewarding shared experience for both pilots and stokers.
