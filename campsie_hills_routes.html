Route Overview

These are excellent rides which take in a number of iconic and demanding climbs, most notably:

  • Tak Ma Doon - Common to both routes
  • Crow Road - Common to both routes
  • Top O' The World - Extended route only

Historical significance:

  • Top O' The World featured in The Milk Race (now Tour of Britain)
  • Crow Road featured in the 2023 UCI World Road Racing Championship course
  • Tak Ma Doon was used in a successful World Everesting record attempt (34 climbs in under 12 hours!)

Start and Finish Point

Location: Car park opposite the Boathouse Bar and Restaurant

Address: Auchinstarry Marina on the south bank of the Forth and Clyde Canal adjacent to the B802 (Howe Road)

Basic Route
40km approximately Moderately Difficult to Difficult

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Start: Exit car park and turn RIGHT onto B802 (Howe Road)
  2. Follow B802 into Kilsyth to junction with A803 (Stirling Road) - Kilsyth Swimming Pool is on the left
  3. Turn RIGHT onto A803 (Stirling Road)
  4. After short distance, turn LEFT onto Tak Ma Doon Road (unclassified road; signs for golf course)
  5. Climb in several steep and lengthy sections past golf course (on left) to car park on right (resting point!)
  6. Continue over summit and descend past Faughlin Reservoir (on right)
  7. Cross River Carron and climb to junction with B818 at Carronbridge
  8. Turn LEFT and follow B818 for several km alongside river then Carron Reservoir
  9. Road descends steeply and bends sharply left to cross Endrick Water
  10. Beyond bridge, B818 bends sharply right towards Fintry - ignore Crow Road for now
  11. Follow B818 to Fintry Inn for lunch
  12. After lunch, return along B818 and carry straight on to join Crow Road
  13. Climb Crow Road for long distance (several wide bends) before steep and fast descent to Lennoxtown
  14. Turn LEFT onto A891 and follow to Milton of Campsie
  15. Turn RIGHT onto B757 (Birdston Road) and follow to junction with A803 in Kirkintilloch
  16. Cross A803 onto Ledgate then turn LEFT onto Eastgate
  17. Take 2ND EXIT at roundabout onto Hillend Road
  18. Leave Hillend Road just before crossing Forth and Clyde Canal and turn LEFT onto NCR754 on north bank
  19. Follow NCR754 until Auchinstarry Marina comes into view on opposite bank
  20. Finish: Leave NCR754 and use B802 to cross canal, then turn LEFT into Marina car park
Extended Route
65km approximately Difficult to Very Difficult

Extended Route Instructions:

  1. Follow Basic Route as far as Carronbridge
  2. Cross B818 to join unclassified road
  3. Carry straight on uphill, ignoring turns left or right, past Loch Coulter
  4. Beyond Muirpark Farm, turn LEFT and follow road to North Third Reservoir (on right)
  5. Near north end of reservoir and just after 'Osprey Hideaways', turn SHARP LEFT onto Top O' The World road
  6. Climb Top O' The World (long and steep climb as name implies)
  7. After climb, road descends to meet B818
  8. Turn RIGHT here to re-join Basic Route

Important Notes and Warnings

  • Weather: Both routes are serious propositions best avoided in poor weather due to remoteness and exposure to elements
  • Tak Ma Doon descent: Road crosses small concrete-lined ford - prominently signposted but care required
  • Facilities: Carron Valley MTB Trails (left of B818 from Carronbridge) - toilets available
  • Lunch stop: Fintry Inn (re-opened June 2022) - good food and brews its own beer
  • Road conditions: Top O' The World surface poor in places, particularly on descent
  • Safety: Blind right-hand bend near bottom of descent to Lennoxtown - care required
  • Alternative route: Strathkelvin Railway Path provides more direct, traffic-free route from Lennoxtown to Kirkintilloch - resurfaced 2022
  • NCR754 chicanes: Several chicanes not tandem-friendly - bikes can be lifted around/over them, or avoid by following A803 east from Kirkintilloch then fork RIGHT onto B8023 to B802
