TTGR55 – Andrew Dalrymple Memorial Ride

Background

For the background to this commemorative ride, see the Andrew Dalrymple page on the TT website.

The ride honours the 1938 record set by Andrew Dalrymple and Chic Dorward, who completed a tandem ride from Kirkcaldy to St Andrews and back in 2h 8m 44s.

Recce Information

Outward leg ridden on 13 March 2025: 52 km in 2h 39m 40s. Navigational missteps added around 4 km / 15 minutes.

Logistics & Lunch

Tandems are transported from DBSC to the START and from FINISH back to DBSC.

Riders return to START by train.

Lunch stop: Village Café, Ceres.
