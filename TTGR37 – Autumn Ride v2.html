Route Notes

The route mainly uses cycle paths and quiet roads, with approximately 1 km of off-road riding through the Logie Estate.

Two crossings of the A985(T) are required. These are best tackled dismounted for safety.

The Logie Estate road is fully metalled (tarmac) but frequently muddy, potholed and puddled due to farm vehicle use. It is rideable with care, and avoids the hazardous “Urquhart Cut” on the A994.

Cold-weather caution: Ice can accumulate in puddles and may be hidden.
