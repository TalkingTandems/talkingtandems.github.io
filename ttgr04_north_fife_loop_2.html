Lunch Options

  • Jamesfield Farm Shop (between Abernethy & Newburgh) — good for larger groups.
  • Buttercup Café, East Port Garage, Newburgh — a TT favourite.

Route Notes

  • Climbs on Basic: Glen Tarkie → Abernethy Glen (cautious descent), Den of Lindores climb towards Auchtermuchty, and a long but gentle grind up Dryside Road back to Glenlomond.
  • Extended (1): Slightly easier overall than Basic by avoiding the Den of Lindores → Auchtermuchty climb.
  • Extended (2): Adds two serious climbs with great descents: Falkland → Leslie via Craigmead, and Auchmuir Bridge → Scotlandwell late in the ride.
