Route Overview

  • Distance: 40km approximately (to be checked)
  • Cumulative Height Gain: tbc
  • Grade: Moderately Difficult to Difficult

The difficulty rating is primarily due to the Falkland to Craigmead climb (long and testing) and the shorter but late-in-the-day climb after Auchmuir Bridge.

Start and Finish Point

Location: Lochend Farm Shop (SE end of Loch Leven)

Postcode: KY13 9JQ

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Start: Lochend Farm Shop
  2. Head to Scotlandwell
  3. Continue through Kinnesswood
  4. Ride to Glen Lomond
  5. Follow Dryside Road to Strathmiglo
  6. Proceed via Easter Cash
  7. Continue to Dunshalt
  8. Then to Freuchie
  9. Head for Newton of Falkland
  10. Arrive at Falkland for lunch
  11. Climb to Craigmead
  12. Descend towards Leslie
  13. Continue to Auchmuir Bridge
  14. Return through Scotlandwell
  15. Finish: Back to Lochend

Lunch Options in Falkland

  • Campbell’s – Very good but often very busy.
  • Hayloft – Also very good but quite small, with a slightly tricky staircase.
  • Pillars of Hercules – Best avoided unless crowds, bland food, daft prices and slow service are your thing…
  • There are also a number of hotels and pubs if beer is the order of the day.

Important Notes and Warnings

  • Season & Weather: This is a tougher route than its modest length suggests and is best avoided in winter or early spring when snow and ice on the Falkland to Leslie leg is common.
  • Highlights: The morning features a fast descent to Strathmiglo via Dryside Road — watch for potholes.
  • Climbs: A tough climb from Falkland to Craigmead, followed by a fast descent to Leslie. The climb after Auchmuir Bridge is also challenging.
