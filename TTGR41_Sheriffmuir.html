Notes & Warnings

  • Recce’d on 31 May 2022 by David Mann, Roy Smith and Louise McLeary.
  • Route includes two excellent pit stops: Braco Coffee Shop and Corrieri’s Café at Causewayhead (closed Tuesdays).
  • Option for pre/post-ride refreshments at the Powmill Milk Bar.
  • A9 crossing at Greenloaning: requires very careful group management for absolute safety.
  • Avoid in winter: high ground at Sheriffmuir and short daylight hours make conditions unsafe.
