TTGR13 – Loch Leven Loops

Two loop options around Loch Leven, ideal for winter riding or poor-weather Plan B.

Basic Route (25 km)

Basic Route
25 km Easy

Step-by-Step

  1. Start: Findatie
  2. → Lochend
  3. → Scotlandwell
  4. → Kinnesswood
  5. → Balgedie
  6. → Milnathort (Heaven Scent – Lunch)
  7. → Kinross
  8. → Gairneybridge
  9. Finish: Findatie

Extended Route (35 km)

Extended Route
35 km Easy to Moderate

Step-by-Step

  1. Start: Findatie
  2. → Lochend
  3. → Scotlandwell
  4. → Kinnesswood
  5. → Balgedie
  6. → Milnathort (Heaven Scent – Lunch)
  7. → Tillywhally
  8. → Dalqueich
  9. → Balado
  10. → Coldrain
  11. → Cleish
  12. Finish: Findatie

Notes

  • These routes are ideal for a short winter’s day or as a Plan B in poor weather; the Basic Route is relatively flat; the Extended Route is more undulating.
  • Both groups ride together to the lunch stop, then split afterward.
