TTGR39 – 4 Braes Ride

Primary Start/Finish: Markinch Rail Station Car Park

Alternative Start/Finish (easy loop): Ladybank Station car park

Main Route – 4 Braes Ride

4 Braes Ride (Main Route)
35 km (approx) Difficult

Step-by-Step Places

  1. Start: Markinch Rail Station Car Park
  2. → Star
  3. → Cadger's Brae (descent)
  4. → Freuchie
  5. → Giffordtown ('5 Ways')
  6. → Ladybank (Bunker Café – lunch)
  7. → Kingskettle
  8. → Annfield Brae (ascent)
  9. → Porter's Brae (ascent)
  10. → Langside Road (descent) – Kennoway (outskirts)
  11. Finish: Markinch Rail Station Car Park

This revised version avoids the now-closed Muddy Boots farm shop and café, so the loop no longer runs via Pitlessie and Balmacolm and is slightly shorter. It remains a fairly tough proposition, with the ascents of Annfield and Porter’s Braes after lunch, followed by a fast descent of Langside Road to the outskirts of Kennoway (beware crosswinds) and a drawn-out counter-climb round the back of Star on the way to the finish. The latter is not particularly difficult, provided the winds are favourable.

Alternative Easy Loop

Ladybank Short Loop
12 km (approx) Easy to Moderately Difficult

Step-by-Step Places

  1. Start: Ladybank Station car park
  2. → Kingskettle
  3. → Freuchie
  4. → Newton of Falkland
  5. → Falkland
  6. → Dunshalt
  7. → Giffordtown ('5 Ways')
  8. → Ladybank
  9. Finish: Ladybank Station car park

Indicative Grade: Easy on a calm day or if the wind is from the East; Easy to Moderately Difficult if the wind is from the West.

Several lunch stop opportunities exist in Falkland, the most practicable usually being the Hayloft Tearoom in Back Wynd, which has been used by TT members in the past. Alternatively, there is a possible coffee stop at Dunshalt village shop if lunch is not required, or if Falkland is very busy (which is often the case).
