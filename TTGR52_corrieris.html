TTGR52 – Corrieri's Ride

A long, hilly loop north of Dunfermline, taking in the Hillfoots, Stirling and a café stop at Corrieri's, with an alternative route starting from Saline.

Main Start/Finish: Home (Dunfermline area)

Alternative Start/Finish: Saline Bus Terminus & Car Park

Notes

  • A variation of the main route was ridden on 30/08/2024: outward via the main route to Corrieri's (~43 km), returning from Dollar via Solsgirth, Saline and Oakley (~40 km).
