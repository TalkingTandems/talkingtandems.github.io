Loch Leven – Dunning Loop (2)

Accessible cycling guide for a challenging loop to Dunning, with two return options: via Glenfarg (Difficult) or via Wicks O’ Baiglie (Very Difficult).

Route Overview

  • Distance: ~72 km
  • Grade: Difficult (via Glenfarg); Very Difficult (via Wicks O’ Baiglie)

Outbound through quiet lanes to Dunning for lunch, then choose your return climb: the steadier Glenfarg or the longer, steeper Wicks O’ Baiglie.

Start and Finish Point

Location: Findatie Car Park (south end of Loch Leven)

Loch Leven – Dunning Loop (2)
~72 km Difficult / Very Difficult

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Start: Findatie
  2. Head to Cleish
  3. Continue through Crook of Devon
  4. Ride via Lendrick Muir
  5. Proceed to Rumbling Bridge
  6. Continue to Yetts O’ Muckhart
  7. Reach Dunning (lunch)
  8. Ride via Invermay
  9. Continue to Forgandenny
  10. Head to Kintillo
  11. Choose return:
    • Glenfarg route
    • Wicks O’ Baiglie route
  12. Continue to Mawcarse
  13. Proceed to Balgedie
  14. Ride through Kinnesswood
  15. Continue to Scotlandwell
  16. Finish: Return to Findatie

Lunch Options

  • The Tee Room at Dunning Golf Club — an excellent lunch venue.

Important Notes and Warnings

  • Glenfarg Ascent: Requires care — avoid bunching — but is not as hazardous as it may seem.
  • Wicks O’ Baiglie: Preferred by some for safety, but longer and much more challenging, with a particularly brutal initial ramp.
  • Related route: TTGR08 is more or less the reverse of this loop: easier around Glenfarg but a much tougher climb over Dunning Glen from the north.
