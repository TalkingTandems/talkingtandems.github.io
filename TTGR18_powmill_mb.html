Route Overview

Eight Powmill Milk Bar rendezvous rides offering a variety of distances and start points, from local loops around Findatie and Cleish to longer challenges from the Shed, Dunfermline, Kirkcaldy and South of the River.

  • All routes use quiet roads where possible, but normal care and road awareness are essential throughout.
  • Longer options (especially “From South of the River”) are very demanding in overall distance.
