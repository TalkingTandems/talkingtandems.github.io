Scenery and points of interest

Starting from Dalgety Bay Sailing Club, this 26-mile circular ride explores a varied corner of West Fife, combining established towns, smaller communities and the coastal settlements along the Firth of Forth. Heading through Hillend and Inverkeithing, the route passes the old naval base area before continuing through Rosyth and Camdean, offering a journey through some of the area's contrasting urban and rural surroundings.

Leaving Rosyth behind, the ride follows Grange Road towards Logie Estate, then continues west through Crossford and Cairneyhill. The route turns back eastwards through Muirside and Crombie, passing through a mixture of residential areas and countryside. As the ride approaches Charleston and Limekilns, the character changes again, with these historic coastal villages providing an attractive contrast to the inland sections.

The final stretch returns through Rosyth and Inverkeithing before joining the Fife Coastal Path for the approach towards Dalgety Bay Sailing Club. With its changing surroundings and mix of inland and coastal locations, the Autumn Ride offers plenty of variety for tandem crews to enjoy together. In autumn, the seasonal changes add another dimension to the experience, with pilots able to share descriptions of the landscape and surroundings with their stokers.

Terrain and cycling difficulty

Although the overall climbing appears relatively modest, tandem crews should be prepared for an undulating journey with occasional short climbs and changes in gradient.
