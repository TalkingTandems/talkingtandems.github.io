TTGR47 – E-Assist Test Route 1

This is a figure-of-8 route with the crossing point at Auchtertool.

It features a mixture of:
• Short, steep climbs
• Long, steady gradients
making it ideal for learning how to get the best from an e-assist bike.

Perfectly doable on a conventional bike — but considerably more challenging.

Start/Finish: DBSC Shed

Distance: ~50 km (pending recce)

Grade: Moderately Difficult → Difficult (e-assist)
Difficult → Very Difficult (conventional bikes)
