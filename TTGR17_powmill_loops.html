TTGR17 – Powmill Loops

THE POWMILL LOOPS - SOMETHING FOR EVERYONE!

A series of loops of widely varying lengths (from 20 kms or less to 50 kms and more ) that all start and finish at Powmill Milk Bar, and which are aimed at getting as many TT members, prospective members and guest riders as possible out on the same day to ride as much (or as little) as they want to.

There will be support vehicles on stand-by throughout the day at the Milk Bar with the ability to transport tandems, solo bikes and riders, so if anyone has technical problems (or seriously overstretches themselves), rescue will be a call from the riders’ mobiles away…

None of the loops is completely flat – they all have some climbing in them – but in general, the shorter the loop, the easier the climbing, and the longer the loop… well, you work it out…!

All of the loops can be ridden in either direction although some are easier (or more difficult) depending on the chosen direction of travel. Wind speed and direction on the day might be the deciding factor.

Many of the loops use sections of the B9097/A977, or B918/A91 all of which are relatively quiet and fairly straight/flat; so - wind direction allowing - there is scope for some relaxed or fast riding in places (you decide!).

The options are many and varied but here are some suggestions:

Choose one of the shorter loops, ride just one lap and call it quits...

Ride 2 (or more) laps of the same loop – possibly in different directions…

Ride two or more different loops, with a lunch stop at PMB between the loops…

Go for one of the longer loops and make a day ride of it…

Have a look at the OS map (Sheet 58) and design a loop to suit your own preferences!

As all of the loops (shown below in ascending order of approx distance) start and finish at the Powmill Milk Bar, refreshments (and loos) will be readily available during opening hours (9am - 5pm; doors locked from 4.30pm).
