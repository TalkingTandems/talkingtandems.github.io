TTGR25 – Dryside, Wicks & Tarkie

Start/Finish: Strathmiglo

Distance & Ascent: To be established (recce required)

Grade: Difficult

Main Loop
Difficult

Step-by-Step Route

  1. Start: START
  2. → Dryside Road
  3. → Gateside
  4. → Arngask
  5. → Glenfarg
  6. → Wicks O' Baiglie
  7. → Kintillo
  8. → Bridge of Earn
  9. → Earn Coffee Shop (Lunch)
  10. → Craigend
  11. → Rhynd
  12. → Elcho
  13. → Bridge of Earn (again)
  14. → Pitkeathly Wells
  15. → Balmanno
  16. → Aberargie
  17. → Abernethy Glen
  18. → Glen Tarkie
  19. Finish: FINISH

Outward Leg Notes

  • Ascent of the Dryside Road is usually into the wind.
  • Care required crossing the busy A91 at Gateside.
  • Long but steady climb from Gateside to Arngask.
  • Wicks O' Baiglie is a fast descent; steepens abruptly near the bottom.

Return Leg Notes

  • Section from the coffee shop to Bridge of Earn uses parts of the old Perth United CC 'Hilly Ten' TT course.
  • Awkward right turn from the A912 into the Rhynd Road.
  • Ascent of Abernethy Glen is tough, especially in the upper section.
  • Glen Tarkie is an exhilarating descent.
  • Care required re-crossing the busy A91 approaching the finish.

Additional Notes

  • The Rhynd Road loop is optional.
