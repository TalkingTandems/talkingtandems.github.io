Scenery and points of interest

Starting and finishing at Leuchars Station, this 47.9 km circular ride explores the varied countryside and communities of north-east Fife. Leaving Leuchars, the route heads through Balmullo, passing Balmullo Quarry before continuing towards Logie and Foodieash. The journey then reaches the historic market town of Cupar, providing a change of setting from the smaller settlements along the way.

From Cupar, the route continues towards the village of Ceres before travelling through New Gilston and Peat Inn. This section takes riders through another part of the Fife countryside, linking villages and rural communities as the route gradually turns back towards the east.

The return journey passes Crossgates and Craigtoun before continuing through Strathkinness and Guardbridge. From Guardbridge, the route heads back to Leuchars Station, completing a varied circuit that combines countryside riding with visits to several distinctive Fife settlements.

  • Balmullo and Balmullo Quarry: Early landmarks as the ride heads away from Leuchars.

  • Ceres: A traditional Fife village providing another recognisable point along the journey.
  • Peat Inn and New Gilston: Rural settlements on the southern part of the circuit.
  • Strathkinness and Guardbridge: The final named communities before returning to Leuchars Station.

Terrain and cycling difficulty

At approximately 47.9 km (29.8 miles), this is a moderately difficult circular ride through north-east Fife. It offers a satisfying outing for tandem crews comfortable with a ride of this length, with a mixture of village approaches, countryside sections and changes of direction. Pilots and stokers should allow time for a comfortable pace and regular opportunities to regroup or rest.
