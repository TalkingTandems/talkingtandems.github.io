TTGR37 – DBSC Training Loop (Extended Directions)

Start/Finish: Dalgety Bay Sailing Club (DBSC)

Approximate Distance: ≈38 km

Indicative Grade: Moderately Difficult

Turn-by-Turn Directions
≈38 km Moderately Difficult

Step-by-Step Directions (Option B)

  1. START – Exit DBSC Car Park and Turn RIGHT
    On to The Wynd
  2. 500 meters – At the Roundabout take second exit (STRAIGHT AHEAD)
    On to Regents Way
  3. 600meters – At the Roundabout take second exit (STRAIGHT AHEAD)
    On to Western Access Road
  4. 750 meters – Turn LEFT at New care home
    On to Harbour Way
  5. 1km – At mini roundabout take second exit (RIGHT)
    Immediately get on to multi-use path alongside Harbour Drive
  6. 1.5kms – Follow cycle path round to the left
    Alongside Hillend By-Pass
  7. 3.5kms – At the end of the cycle path pass through a chicane and exit (LEFT) on to Hillend Road
    Heading towards Inverkeithing; High School on the right
  8. 3.5kms – Follow Hillend Road round to the right and follow to the T-junction
    Becomes Boreland Road
  9. 5.5kms – At the T-junction turn LEFT
    Into Church Street
  10. 6kms – Go STRAIGHT AHEAD into High Street and then downhill into Hope Street
    Pass under railway with Park & Ride on the right
  11. 7.75kms – At the roundbout, take the SECOND exit, passing under the A90
    Care required; watch for traffic from the right
  12. 7.8kms – At the traffic lights (under the A90) follow the road round to the right then take the first LEFT
    Into King Malcolm Drive
  13. 8kms – At the traffic lights go STRAIGHT AHEAD
  14. 8.2kms – At the roundabout, take the FIRST exit
    Into Milne Road.
  15. 8.3kms – Go STRAIGHT AHEAD into Barham Road
    This is the old Naval Base
  16. 8.6kms – At the roundabout, take the THIRD exit
    Into Millenium Avenue
  17. 8.75kms – At the roundabout, take the SECOND exit
    Into Castle Road
  18. 9.1kms – At the roundabout, take the SECOND exit
    Continuing on Castle Road
  19. 9.3kms – At the roundabout, take the SECOND exit
    Into King's Road This is the junction with the busy A985; extra care required
  20. 9.3kms – Follow King's Road downhill
    Allotments on the left; Primary School on the right
  21. 9.8kms – At the roundabout, take the FIRST exit
    Into Park Road (West)
  22. 9.9kms – After just a few meters, turn RIGHT
    Into Primrose Avenue
  23. 10.1kms – Turn RIGHT
    Into Anderson Lane
  24. 10.2kms – At the junction, turn LEFT
    Into Primrose Lane
  25. 10.2kms – Follow Primrose Lane to junction then turn RIGHT
    Into Grange Road. This is an awkward turn; extra care required
  26. 10.5kms – Follow Grange Road all the way to Dunfermline
    Becomes Elgin Street as it approaches the T-junction
  27. 13.8kms – At the traffic lights, turn LEFT downhill then shortly afterwards, make a RIGHT turn into Milton Green/Lover's Loan
    This can be an awkward turn, if there is traffic behind as well as oncoming
  28. 14.2kms – Where the road bends round to the right (Coal Road) go STRAIGHT AHEAD into the Logie Estate (The Logie Estate road, despite its outward appearance, is in fact a metalled (tarmac) road throughout its length; however it is heavily used by farm vehicles and, as a consequence, is muddy and festooned with potholes and puddles in places. It is nevertheless rideable with care, and allows the potentially dangerous 'Urquhart Cut' on the A994 to be avoided.)
    It is necessary to dismount to get the bikes past the (sometimes) locked barrier.
  29. 14.2kms – Follow the rough road through the Logie Estate avoiding the mud and potholes/puddles where practicable, with three 90 degree turns; first a LEFT, then a RIGHT then another RIGHT
    leading to the exit from the estate on to Crossford Main Street
  30. 15.5kms – Turn LEFT out of Logie Estate
    on to Main Street
  31. 15.5kms – Follow Main Street (STRAIGHT AHEAD at traffic lights) until the junction with Douglas Drive
    Pitfirrane Golf Course on the left
  32. 17kms – Cross the road to get on to the cycle path
  33. 17kms – Follow the cycle path to its end at Cairneyhill
    Care required when re-joining the main carriageway
  34. 19.5kms – Follow Main Street through the village and turn LEFT immediately after the Hairdressers
    Into Muirside Road
  35. 21.5kms – At the end of Muirside Road, fork RIGHT
    Into Muirside Lane
  36. 22kms – Follow Muirside lane until it reaches the A985
  37. 22kms – Cross the A985 and get on to the cycle path going towards Crombie
    It will probably be safer to dismount and cross the A985 on foot
  38. 23.5kms – Follow the cycle path, crossing the minor road from Crombie Point, until the junction with Farm Road is reached
  39. 23.5kms – At the junction turn RIGHT
    Into Farm Road
  40. 24.5kms – Follow Farm Road until it meets Ordnance Road, then turn RIGHT and immediately LEFT
    Into Little Foothorn
  41. 24.75kms – Follow Little Foothorn until it is possible to re-join the cycle path alongside the A985 and follow the cycle path until it reaches the road leading to Charleston
  42. 25.75kms – Turn RIGHT into this road and follow it all the way to Limekilns Promenade
    Bumpy descent on cobblestones just before reaching the Promenade
  43. 27.25kms – Follow the Promenade past the car park and harbour then follow Church Street/Dunfermline Road uphill to the junction with the A985
  44. 29kms – At the junction, turn RIGHT on to the A985 and follow this road until it is possible to make a further RIGHT turn into the western access to Rosyth Dockyard
    This is only safe if the traffic is light. If the A985 is busy (which it often is) it will be safer to walk the bikes across the road and ride along the pavement until the Pattiesmuir Road is reached, dismounting here and re-crossing the A985 on foot to reach the cycle path which is then followed until it meets the western access road
  45. 29.1kms – At the mini-roundabout on the western access road, take the FIRST exit on to Hilton Road and follows this all the way to the next roundabout
  46. 30kms – At the roundabout, take the SECOND exit into Ferry Toll Road which is then followed to the traffic lights
  47. 31.8kms – At the traffic lights, turn LEFT and go STRAIGHT AHEAD until the roundabout is reached
  48. 32kms – At the roundabout PASS UNDER the A90 then take the exit for Inverkeithing
    Park & Ride on the left
  49. 32.3kms – Follow Hope Street uphill until just after the Square, then turn RIGHT
    Into King Street
  50. 33.3kms – Follow King Street downhill then turn RIGHT into Commercial Street and fork LEFT into Preston Crescent
  51. 33.6kms – Follow Preston Crescent to its end then go STRAIGHT AHEAD on to the Fife Coastal Path and follow it to St David's Harbour
    Fife Stevedores on the right
  52. 35kms – From the roundabout at St David's Harbour follow the signs to continue on the COASTAL PATH via the Bridges and Lumsdaine Drive to Dalgety Bay
    In order to avoid dismounting, take the LEFT FORK in the gravel path leading to The Bridges
  53. 35.2kms – Athe end of the gravel path, TURN RIGHT on to The Bridges
    Visibility at the turn obscured by bushes; care required
  54. 35.4kms – Follow The Bridges uphill until it is possible to get back on to the Fife Coastal Path
  55. 35.6kms – Follow the coastal path for a short distance until a LEFT turn immediately followed by a RIGHT turn gives access to Lumsdaine Drive
    Tricky on a tandem; care required
  56. 35.8kms – Follow Lumsdaine Drive to the junction with Moray Way
  57. 36.5kms – At the junction turn RIGHT on to Moray Way and follow it to the roundabout
  58. 37.5kms – At the roundabout take the THIRD exit
    On to The Wynd
  59. 38kms – Follow The Wynd downhill until the road bends to the right then turn LEFT to Finish
    DBSC car park
