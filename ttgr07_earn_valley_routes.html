TTGR07 – Earn Valley Routes

Map: OS Sheet 58 (Perth & Alloa)

Start/Finish: Earn Coffee Shop car park, just north of Bridge of Earn. Alternative: Bridge of Earn Institute, Station Road (slightly shorter).

All routes share the same outward leg to the lunch stop at The Tee Room, Dunning Golf Club. The Basic route stays in the lower Earn Valley; the Extended routes follow the Tay downstream later on.

Basic Route
32 km Easy to Moderately Difficult

Route

Bridge of Earn → Forgandenny → Dunning (via Invermay Rd) (lunch) → Dupplin Brae → Aberdalgie → Kirkton of Mailer → Craigend → Bridge of Earn.

Terrain

  • Pre‑lunch: mainly flat with one long (but not hard) climb before Forgandenny.
  • Post‑lunch: lumpier after crossing the Earn, with some short, testing climbs.
  • Easier option: From Dunning return via Forteviot, then either retrace the outward leg to the finish or take an undulating road via Pitkeathly Wells, Dron and Kintillo.
Extended Route (1)
42 km Moderately Difficult

Route

As for Basic until just before Aberdalgie, then: Milton of Aberdalgie → Perth → Rhynd → Bridge of Earn.

This follows part of the old coaching route into Perth (from the Glasgow direction) and sections of the former Perth United CC “Hilly Ten” TT course. Prior knowledge of the route through Darnhall and Craigie (Perth) is useful.

Extended Route (2)
70 km Moderate – Difficult

Route

As for Basic to lunch, then: Dunning → Aberuthven → Kinkell Bridge → Trinity Gask → Balgowan → Tibbermore → Ruthvenfield → Perth → Rhynd → Bridge of Earn.

  • Optional: Skip the Rhynd loop (~10 km) if time is pressing.
  • Second stop: Possible at Tibbermore — Gloagburn Farm (excellent but often very busy; if needed, divert to Dobbies GC, East Huntingtower).
  • Perth caution: Prior knowledge of the route through Perth is essential to avoid major works on Tay St/Shore Rd.

General Notes

  • All routes start/finish at Earn Coffee Shop car park; the Bridge of Earn Institute alternative shortens routes slightly.
  • All outward legs are shared to the lunch stop at Dunning Golf Club (The Tee Room).
  • Extended routes track the River Tay downstream in their later stages.
