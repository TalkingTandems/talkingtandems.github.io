TTGR38 – Dunning Glen & Glendevon

Start/Finish: Powmill Milk Bar

Distance: 48 km

Ascent: 550 m

Grade: Difficult (on account of the amount of climbing)

Main Route
48 km / 550 m Difficult

Step-by-Step Route

  1. Start: Powmill Milk Bar
  2. → Rumbling Bridge
  3. → Yetts O' Muckhart
  4. → Dunning
  5. → Auchterarder Station (site of)
  6. → Duchally
  7. → Glenhead
  8. → Glendevon
  9. → Yetts O' Muckhart
  10. → Rumbling Bridge
  11. Finish: Powmill Milk Bar

Route Notes

This relatively short but nevertheless worthwhile route features four long but well-graded climbs which together account for at least 30% of the total distance. These are:

  • Rumbling Bridge → Yetts O' Muckhart
  • Dunning Glen (northbound — the easier direction)
  • Auchterarder Station → Duchally
  • Gleneagles (southbound)

There are several long and enjoyable descents, but the descent of Dunning Glen requires great care due to sharp bends with poor sight lines.

Refreshments and toilets available at The Tee Room in Dunning Golf Club, as well as at the start/finish at Powmill Milk Bar.
