Scenery and points of interest

The Hangover Ride is a gentle 16mile outing through West Fife, starting and finishing at Dalgety Bay Sailing Club. Designed as an easy ride, it offers a manageable distance for cyclists looking to enjoy some time together without committing to a longer day in the saddle.

The route passes through the familiar West Fife towns of Inverkeithing and Rosyth before heading towards the village of Crossford, offering an enjoyable journey through this part of the Kingdom of Fife.

From Crossford, the route turns back towards Rosyth and Inverkeithing, eventually returning to Dalgety Bay Sailing Club. With its straightforward out-and-back character and familiar locations along the way, the Hangover Ride is a pleasant option for a relaxed club outing, allowing pilots and stokers to enjoy the journey and conversation together.

Route highlights

  • Dalgety Bay Sailing Club: The familiar starting and finishing point for Talking Tandems rides.
  • Inverkeithing: An established Fife town visited on both the outward and return journeys.
  • Rosyth: A key point along the route, linking the eastern and western sections of the ride.
  • Crossford: The westernmost named village on the route, marking the turnaround towards Dalgety Bay.
  • The return journey: A chance to revisit the route's familiar locations from the opposite direction.
