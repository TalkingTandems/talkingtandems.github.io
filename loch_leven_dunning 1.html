Route Overview

  • Distance: ~70 km
  • Grade: Difficult

This route features a north–south crossing of Dunning Glen — the harder direction — making for a tough start to the homeward leg after lunch.

Start and Finish Point

Location: Findatie Car Park (south end of Loch Leven)

Loch Leven – Dunning Loop
~70 km Difficult

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Start: Findatie
  2. Head to Scotlandwell
  3. Continue through Kinnesswood
  4. Ride to Balgedie Toll
  5. Proceed to Mawcarse
  6. Continue into Glenfarg
  7. Climb the Wicks O’ Baiglie
  8. Descend towards Pitkeathly Wells
  9. Continue to Forgandenny
  10. Reach Dunning (lunch)
  11. Head to Yetts O’ Muckhart
  12. Ride via Rumbling Bridge
  13. Stop at Powmill (refreshment stop)
  14. Continue to Aldie
  15. Proceed to Cleish
  16. Finish: Return to Findatie

Lunch Options

  • The Tee Room at Dunning Golf Club — an excellent lunch venue.

Important Notes and Warnings

  • Dunning Glen: The north → south crossing is the tougher of the two directions and makes for a stiff start after lunch.
  • Second stop: A suggested refreshment stop at Powmill on the homeward leg.
