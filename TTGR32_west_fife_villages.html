Points of Interest

  • 1: Hill of Beath – Statue of Jim Baxter, legendary Rangers & Scotland player.
  • 2: Lumphinnans – Known as 'Little Moscow'; includes Gagarin Way.
  • 3: Glencraig – Fife Cycle Park.
  • 4: Kelty / Lochore Meadows – Reclaimed mining area; miner statue.
  • 5: Townhill Park – Billy Liddell memorial & mining history.
  • 6: Clackmannan – ‘Stone of Manau’ in town square.
  • 7: Kincardine – 1936 swing bridge; Scottish Police College.
  • 8: Culross – Historic village, Outlander filming location.
  • 9: Low Valleyfield – Colliery memorial plaque.
  • 10: Charleston & Limekilns – Old harbour & limestone quarrying.
  • 11: Naval Base – Site of German fleet salvage; laid‑up submarines.
  • 12: Inverkeithing – Ancient Royal Burgh; stone quarries for London paving.
