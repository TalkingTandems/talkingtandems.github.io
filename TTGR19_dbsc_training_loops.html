TTGR19 – DBSC Training Loops

Start/Finish (both loops): Dalgety Bay Sailing Club (DBSC)

Short Loop
6 km Easy

Short Loop Directions

  1. Exit DBSC and turn RIGHT on to The Wynd.
  2. At the roundabout take the first exit (LEFT) on to Moray Way South.
  3. Follow Moray Way South and then Moray Way North until the roundabout.
  4. Take the second exit at the roundabout (AHEAD) and continue on Moray Way North.
  5. Follow Moray Way North until the next roundabout.
  6. Take the second exit at the roundabout (RIGHT) on to Moray Way South.
  7. Follow Moray Way South until the roundabout.
  8. Take the first exit at the roundabout (LEFT) on to The Wynd.
  9. Follow The Wynd downhill and, as the road bends to the right, turn LEFT into the DBSC car park.
Long Loop
12.5 km Easy to Moderately Difficult

Long Loop Directions

  1. Exit DBSC and turn RIGHT on to The Wynd.
  2. At the roundabout take the first exit (LEFT) on to Moray Way South.
  3. Turn LEFT on to Lumsdane Drive.
  4. Follow Lumsdane Drive almost to its end then turn sharp LEFT and immediately sharp RIGHT (tricky) to get on to a short section of path which leads to The Bridges.
  5. Follow The Bridges downhill then a short distance uphill, turning sharp LEFT just beyond a hedge (awkward – watch out for the remains of a bollard) on to a short section of off-road path that leads to the car park at St David’s Bay.
  6. Exit the car park and follow the road round the roundabout until it is possible to exit LEFT on to the Fife Coastal Path.
  7. Follow the path to its end at Preston Crescent.
  8. Follow Preston Crescent to the junction.
  9. Turn RIGHT at the junction and climb a short hill to another junction.
  10. Turn RIGHT on to Alma Street and go downhill then steeply uphill until it is possible to go AHEAD on to Hillend Road (care required – traffic from right or left has right of way).
  11. Follow Hillend Road past the schools then fork RIGHT on to the cycle path before the traffic lights.
  12. Follow the cycle path alongside the A921, turning RIGHT alongside Harbour Drive.
  13. Leave the cycle path to get on to Harbour Drive and follow this via two roundabouts (second exit, AHEAD, at each) to Link Road.
  14. Turn LEFT on to Link Road and follow this downhill then steeply uphill to the junction.
  15. Turn LEFT on to Moray Way North and follow this to the roundabout.
  16. Take the second exit at the roundabout (AHEAD) and continue on Moray Way North.
  17. Follow Moray Way North until the roundabout.
  18. Take the second exit at the roundabout (RIGHT) on to Moray Way South.
  19. Follow Moray Way South until the roundabout.
  20. Take the first exit at the roundabout (LEFT) on to The Wynd.
  21. Follow The Wynd downhill and, as the road bends to the right, turn LEFT into the DBSC car park.

Notes

These loops can be ridden separately or sequentially to provide a distance suited to the abilities of individual tandem teams.

Refreshments and toilets are available at Louie Brown’s / Hugo’s Bistro.
